Information and Communication Technology Class Program

Introduction

The ICT Program is designed to equip students with essential skills in modern digital Technology. Through hands-on projects, students will exlore fundational concepts of computer networks, Information systems, and sofware egineering. this comprehensive Program ensures that learners understand how technology impacts daily life and industries worldwide, while also building teamwork and analytical capabilities

Furthermore, this course focuses heavily on practical web development and database management. Students will learn to design interactive websites, manage data struktures, and solve complex digital problems. By the and of this program, students will be prepared to create their own digital solution and pursue advanced studies in technology
